2. The visual design we're building

Visual direction examples. Final website imagery will prioritize your real business photographs.
Design rules
- Large photographs integrated directly into the page layout, not just rectangular stock images.
- A technician working on a door dominates the hero.
- Alternating full-width image sections, small supporting photos and editorial typography.
- Service cards use close-up photographs of actual work.
- Product cards use consistent, high-quality product photography.
- Subtle scroll animations and image reveals, with reduced-motion support.
- Strong Call and WhatsApp buttons without excessive promotional clutter.
We can reproduce the reference's design principles without making an exact copy.

6. Products & pricing
A carefully presented catalogue, not a conventional online store.
Product category	Display

Lock cylinders and locks	Relevant starting tier
Batteries and accessories	Relevant starting tier

Every product tile will feature an actual product image, short title, a starting price such as ₹399 onwards* or ₹999 onwards*, and direct enquiry CTAs.

We will not assign ₹399 or ₹999 to a product unless that price is genuinely available for an option in that product category. That avoids customers being misled by starting prices.

The price disclaimer will explain that the actual charge depends on the item, parts, required work, and any doorstep or emergency charges.
