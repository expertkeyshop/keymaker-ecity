Privacy & cookies.

This page explains what happens when you visit this website, call us, or message us on WhatsApp. It is written to be read, not to hide behind legal language.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Who we are

Key Maker & Locksmith | Electronic City is a local key-cutting and locksmith shop at Gollahalli Main Road, Siraj Layout, Electronic City Phase 1, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560100, India. You can reach us on +91 97623 06303.

This website is an information and contact page. It does not create accounts, take payments, or collect information through a contact form.

Information we may receive

  • If you call or message: your phone number, the message you send, and any details you choose to share, such as a vehicle model, key type, lock problem or location. WhatsApp and your mobile network handle that message under their own privacy terms.
  • If you click a map or review link: Google may receive your request and use its own cookies or identifiers. We do not control Google's processing.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider may record standard request information such as an IP address, browser type, requested page and time for security and reliability.
  • Analytics, only with your consent: Google Analytics 4 tells us things such as which pages are viewed, approximate region, device type and how visitors arrive. We use it to improve the site. We do not use it to identify you personally.

Cookies and analytics

The site loads Google Analytics only after you choose Accept analytics. If you decline, or close the page without choosing, the analytics script is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set by us.

When accepted, Google Analytics may set cookies such as _ga and _ga_<container-id>, usually for up to two years, to distinguish a browser from other browsers. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store visitors' full IP addresses.

Your choice is remembered in your browser's local storage under the key km-analytics-consent. It is not sent to our server. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using Cookie settings, or by clearing site data in your browser.

Google Analytics is provided by Google. Its handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

How we use information

  • To answer your call or message and discuss a key, lock, vehicle or on-site visit.
  • To confirm availability, compatibility, price, arrival time and any travel charges.
  • To keep the website secure and working, and to understand which pages are useful.
  • To meet legal or accounting obligations that apply to the business.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your phone number or message content for advertising audiences.

When information is shared

We share information only where it is needed: with our hosting provider for the site, with Google if you accept analytics or open a Google Maps/review link, and with WhatsApp/Meta if you choose to message us. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety and rights of the business, our customers or the public.

How long we keep it

Call and message details are kept only as long as needed to respond, complete the work, and meet legal or accounting requirements. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in our Google Analytics property and is then deleted or aggregated. Hosting logs are kept for the hosting provider's standard security period.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete information that we do not need to keep. You can also withdraw analytics consent at any time. To make a request, call or message +91 97623 06303 with enough detail for us to find your message.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can raise the matter with the relevant data protection authority in India. Nothing on this page limits a right you have under applicable law.

Children

This website and our services are intended for adults arranging key or locksmith work. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this page

If our tools or practices change, we will update this page and its date. The current version always applies to the live website.
