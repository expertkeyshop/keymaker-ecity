Where is your shop?

We are on Gollahalli Main Road, Siraj Layout, Electronic City Phase 1, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560100. Customers can visit the shop for key making and locksmith services.

Are you open 24 hours?

Yes. We are available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. Call or WhatsApp us to discuss the service you need.

Do you visit customers?

Yes. We provide on-site services across all 20 areas listed on our profile, including locations 10 km or more from the shop. Share your exact address to confirm availability and travel charges.

Which keys can you duplicate?

We duplicate supported house, office, shop and cupboard keys, and make keys for compatible cars and bikes. Bring your original key where available so we can check the correct blank and cutting requirements.

Do you program car keys and key fobs?

Yes, for compatible vehicle models. Share the make, model, year and existing key details so we can confirm the suitable key or fob, programming requirements and quote.

Can you help with lockouts or damaged locks?

Yes. We offer lockout assistance for authorised owners and occupants, plus repair and replacement of compatible locks. Call with your location and details of the problem so we can assess the job.

How much do services cost?

Prices depend on the key or lock type, vehicle compatibility, required parts, programming, and any travel or installation. Contact us with the job details for a quote before work begins.

Which payments do you accept?

We accept cash, UPI and bank transfers through IMPS or NEFT. We do not currently accept card payments.
